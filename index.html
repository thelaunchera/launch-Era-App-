FAQ

FAQ

How do I share the app with a worker?

Go to Team, add the worker, then tap Share worker link on their card. Workers do not need a password.

Can a worker see my revenue or billing?

No. Workers do not see owner reports, invoices, pricing, billing, permissions or integrations.

What happens when a quote is accepted?

The app creates or updates the client, schedules the job and prepares a draft invoice automatically.

Can clients book without creating an account?

Yes. The public booking and quote-request flow is designed for clients to use without an account.

How do I remove someone’s access?

Open Owner settings → Access & Permissions and remove their app access. Their job history stays in the business records.

How do owners and admins sign in?

Owners and admins sign in with their approved email address and password. If you forget your password, use Forgot password? to reset it securely.
