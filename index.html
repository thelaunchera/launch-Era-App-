FAQ

Common questions

How do I share the app with a coworker?

Go to Team → Share App Access. Enter their email, choose Admin or Coworker, then send them the invite link.

Can a coworker see my revenue or billing?

No. Coworkers do not see owner reports, invoices, pricing, billing, permissions or integrations.

What happens when a quote is accepted?

The app creates or updates the client, schedules the job and prepares a draft invoice automatically.

Can clients book without creating an account?

Yes. The public booking and quote-request flow is designed for clients to use without an account.

How do I remove someone’s access?

Open Owner Admin → Access & Permissions and remove their app access. Their job history stays in the business records.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen and follow the email reset link.
